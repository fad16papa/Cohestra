- **Sequential story boundaries** — Each story deferred the next layer explicitly; scope stayed focused.

- **Belt-and-suspenders on exports** — Explicit predicates on top of EF filters; nested-collection gaps closed in CR.
- **SM-1 CI gate hardened** — Rejects zero-match filters, skipped tests, and vacuous-negative-only suites.

- **Middleware alone was insufficient** — Multiple consumer paths still global until review patches landed.

- **SM-1 coverage is minimum, one-directional** — A JWT ↛ B activity only; Epic 15 surfaces not extended (see Epic 15 retro action item).
- **Branch protection not wired** — Workflow steps exist; GitHub required-check wiring deferred to ops.
