## North star

Make somebody mentally move their business into Cohestra before they have signed up.

Cinema job = pre-signup mental occupancy of their club inside Cohestra — not feature education, not brochure polish.

- Every room must feel already furnished with *their* week. If the visitor still feels like a tourist, the replica failed.
- Mental-move-in test is the gate for every decision: keep only what accelerates imagining their club inside Cohestra; kill anything that keeps them outside looking at a demo.
- Believability requires continuity of one club's living data across all 6 tabs + UI grade of real app pages.
- Fix is systemic (density + media + transition), not local polish.

Progressive unlock: Website (I see my club) → Clients (people) → Activities (doing) → Follow-up (losing) → Analytics (working) → AI (what to do).

## Club-fact voice + demo-SaaS kill list

### Club-fact voice

Speak Monday operational sentences from the demo dataset — density filled with meaning, not widgets for the camera.

Examples of voice (room-scoped; do not dump all in Website):

- 17 waiting follow-up
- Friday likely at capacity
- 8 first-timers not returned
- Referrals = most loyal members
- Website first truth only: Friday's event filling up / 34 going · 8 spots left

Believability = specific operational truth from demo dataset, not aesthetic SaaS theater.

### Demo-SaaS kill list (anti-SaaS manifesto)

Forbidden in cinema:

- Floating glass cards
- Giant gradients
- Three-widget photo dashboards
- Acme names
- Meaningless graphs
- Canned %
- Decorative animations
- Sawdust slogans (Powerful CRM / AI-powered / Seamless)
- Soft marketing prose competing with the frame
- Reintroducing glass/gradient/Acme theater under the guise of "dense UI"

Assumption flips (sparse→dense Monday UI, full roster, photo place, room cut, frame-dominant, one-club story, app-grade chrome, filled proof) are kept **only if** filtered through club-fact voice.

## Structural steals (restaurant FOH/BOH, ER triage)

Steal is structural, not visual.

### Restaurant FOH / BOH

- Dining room = Website (FOH experience; guest never sees "hotel/restaurant software" first).
- Swinging door → BOH = Clients / Activities / Follow-up / Analytics / AI.
- Front of house sells experience; back of house proves operation — never show the whole kitchen at once.
- AI = chef's-eye view of what to do next, grounded in rooms already seen.
- Cinema seek = walking through swinging doors; each pill is a station revealed only when meaningful.

### ER triage

- System surfaces who/what needs action now; not everything equal attention.
- Clients = waiting-room chart / context (don't scream).
- Follow-up = ER bay / control room (triage hardest first).
- AI = attending physician order / resolve urgency — not omniscient early.
- Clients subtle states vs Follow-up ruthless Due now / At risk / Opportunity / Healthy hierarchy.

## Dials + red line (suspiciously real)

Dial = how close to inhabited Monday screen — not more stuff. Stop before design-tourism metaphors.

| Dial | Target (11) |
|---|---|
| 1 Data density | 25–40 clients, full activity calendar, populated queues — purpose rows only; operational pressure not wallpaper |
| 2 Continuity | Five characters traceable end-to-end across all rooms; same names / timestamps / capacities / statuses / sources |
| 3 Fidelity | Real software chrome: search / filters / counts / timestamps / chips / breadcrumbs / tables / side panels — do not beautify away ops UI |
| 4 Imperfection | No-response, missing phone, cancelled, no-show, waitlist — perfect data smells demo |
| 5 Cause-and-effect | Each room modifies understanding of previous — causal chain Website→…→AI |
| 6 Copy almost zero | Replica finishes the sentence; kill marketing prose on mounts |

**Red line:** Max operational truth + max hierarchy; high decorative density forbidden.

**Target feel:** Suspiciously real — a five-minute hallucination that you already run the club on Cohestra.

Push Continuity until break: nothing important exists in only one room; fingerprints everywhere.

## Seed dataset contract

Design five character arcs before designing frames. Continuity QA is a product acceptance test for DemoClub seed — write the five arc fixtures before any mount polish.

### Two-class data

| Class | Contract |
|---|---|
| Anchor | 5 people + ~3 activities at 100% continuity across rooms |
| Ambient | 25–50 statistically coherent people/rows; no bespoke arcs |

Density band for Clients roster: 25–40 (within Ambient + Anchor total).

### Anchor ×5 arcs

1. **Maya** — healthy path
2. **Daniel** — leak / at-risk
3. **Priya** — referral opportunity
4. **Marcus** — messy incomplete / no-show; AI cannot WhatsApp (missing phone)
5. **Sarah** — regular / referrer economic mechanism

Named continuity people also used in Website→Clients reveal beat include Maya / Sarah / Daniel among the 34.

### Number assertions (DB, not marketing)

- 34 going = 34 registrations against 42 capacity (8 spots left)
- 17 need attention = 17 records
- AI uses no fantasy dataset — only what seed can prove
- Capacities, timestamps, statuses, sources identical wherever the same fact appears

### Reverse-chain QA

Walk backward: AI → Analytics → Follow-up → Activity → Client → acquisition.

If the chain snaps, the cinema is lying. Marketing claims become database assertions; cause-and-effect comes almost free once continuity holds.

## Shipping gate (5 truths)

Fail any = no ship.

1. **Product** — replicas of real/committed product surfaces only; AI gated as above.
2. **Dataset** — one continuous DemoClub seed; Anchor×5 + Ambient; number assertions hold.
3. **Continuity** — fingerprints across rooms; reverse-chain QA passes; no world reset.
4. **Business** — club-fact voice; operational hierarchy; mental-move-in of a real club week.
5. **Mental-move-in** — visitor imagines their club inside Cohestra before signup; tourist feel = fail.

Cinema may compose, never invent.

## Cinema composition rules

1. **May compose, never invent** — no fake functionality; no world the product cannot inhabit Monday.
2. **Path B replicas, not live routes** — high-fidelity presentational replicas that look like real functional pages; not live operator routes. Squint test required.
3. **FeelingCopy caption-only** — ProductFrame is the proof; copy is caption; frame-dominant; copy dial almost zero.
4. **Website barely Cohestra** — customer brand (Harbourline Social Club inhabited public face); other five rooms = Cohestra machinery behind the door.
5. **House-tour seek order locked** — Website → Clients → Activities → Follow-up → Analytics → Cohestra AI; do not remount old order under new labels.
6. **One seed, six rooms** — continuous living data; cause-and-effect mandatory.
7. **No demo-SaaS theater** — kill list enforced; flips only through club-fact voice.
8. **Triage discipline** — urgency hierarchy + history explanation; never red paint for camera.
9. **Pills are chapters** — outcome-oriented naming allowed (Reports→Analytics); Campaigns inside Follow-up; not full product nav.
10. **Suspiciously real** — dials at 11 within red line; decorative density forbidden.
