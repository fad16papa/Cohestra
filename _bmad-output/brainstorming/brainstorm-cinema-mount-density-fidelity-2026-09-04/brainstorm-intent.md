# Intent: Live Proof Cinema — Suspiciously Real House Tour

## Problem

`/#crm` cinema fails authenticity: sparse mounts, dummy data, thin postcard UI, and soft seeks read as a fake brochure — not a Monday operator workspace. Fix is systemic (density + media + continuity + transitions), not local polish. Job is pre-signup mental occupancy: make a club operator imagine their business already living in Cohestra.

## Outcome

Six high-fidelity presentational replicas (not live operator routes) that pass the squint test as Monday UI. Progressive house tour: customer-brand Website (FOH) → Cohestra machinery rooms (BOH). One continuous DemoClub seed; every claim is a DB assertion. Target: suspiciously real — five-minute hallucination that you already run the club on Cohestra.

## Frozen decisions (bullets)

- Replicas of real/committed product pages — never invent cinema-only worlds; cinema may compose, not invent.
- Pill order is narrative house tour — do not remap onto old feature-carousel order.
- Website barely looks like Cohestra (customer brand / FOH); other five rooms = Cohestra behind the door.
- One continuous seeded club across all six rooms — no world reset between seeks.
- Progressive unlock: Website → Clients → Activities → Follow-up → Analytics → Cohestra AI; don't burn four reveals in the lobby.
- Club-fact voice only (operational truths); Anti-SaaS: no glass cards, giant gradients, Acme names, canned %, decorative animations, sawdust slogans.
- ProductFrame is the proof; copy is caption — almost zero marketing prose on mounts.
- Urgency from dataset, never red paint; imperfect data required (no-response, missing phone, cancel, no-show, waitlist).
- Triage hardest in Follow-up; Clients = context; AI = resolve urgency (not omniscient early).
- Campaigns capabilities live inside Follow-up job; pill labels may be outcome-oriented (Reports → Analytics).
- Mental-move-in test gates every decision: keep only what accelerates imagining their club inside Cohestra.
- Page map: Website=`/dashboard/website`; Clients=`/clients`; Activities=`/activities`; Follow-up=client follow-up + campaigns; Analytics=`/reports` + dashboard reporting; AI=committed intelligence surface (not necessarily `/ai`).
- Shipping gate = five truths (product, dataset, continuity, business, mental-move-in); fail any = no ship.
- Cohestra AI = MUST capability gated by real product surface — build product truth first or hold the chapter.

## Six chapters (order, pill label, product source, prove, not-show)

1. **Website** — Pride / FOH entry  
   - Source: `/dashboard/website`  
   - Prove: inhabited Ikigai Social Club public face — venue photo, THIS WEEK (Friday filling: 34 going · 8 spots left / 42 cap), community faces; feels like *their* club.  
   - Not-show: KPI/CRM/AI overlay, mint void, Cohestra chrome, other club facts reserved for later rooms.

2. **Clients** — know everyone  
   - Source: `/clients`  
   - Prove: the 34 become people (Maya/Sarah/Daniel…); subtle states New / At risk / Needs attention / Returning / Member; 17 waiting follow-up as living roster context.  
   - Not-show: screaming triage, full kitchen dump, equal-attention noise.

3. **Activities** — run it  
   - Source: `/activities`  
   - Prove: operational run (QR/attendance, calendar, capacities continuous with Website regs).  
   - Not-show: decorative event cards, postcard mocks, reset of Website numbers.

4. **Follow-up** — don't lose people  
   - Source: client follow-up + campaigns  
   - Prove: ER control room — ruthless Due now / At risk / Opportunity / Healthy; why-marked history; campaigns inside this job.  
   - Not-show: omniscient AI, red-paint urgency without cause, early resolve.

5. **Analytics** — know what's happening  
   - Source: `/reports` + dashboard reporting  
   - Prove: club-fact operational truth from same seed (e.g. capacity pressure, return/referral economics).  
   - Not-show: meaningless graphs, canned %, Acme theater.

6. **Cohestra AI** — what to do first  
   - Source: committed intelligence surface only  
   - Prove: highest-leverage next action grounded in rooms already seen + Review N people CTA; respects seed limits (e.g. no WhatsApp Marcus without phone).  
   - Not-show: chatbot theater, clairvoyant %, cinema-only AI, inventing actions seed can't support.

## DemoClub seed contract (anchors Maya/Daniel/Priya/Marcus/Sarah + ambient rules + number assertions)

**Two-class data**
- **Anchor** (100% continuous): 5 people + ~3 activities — fingerprints in every relevant room.
- **Ambient**: 25–50 clients, statistically coherent, no bespoke arcs; purpose rows only (operational pressure, not wallpaper).

**Five anchors**
- **Maya** — healthy path.
- **Daniel** — leak / at-risk.
- **Priya** — referral opportunity.
- **Marcus** — messy incomplete / no-show; missing phone → AI cannot WhatsApp.
- **Sarah** — regular / referrer; economic mechanism (referrals = most loyal).

**Continuity rules**
- Same names, timestamps, capacities, statuses, sources end-to-end.
- Nothing important exists in only one room.
- Imperfection propagates (no-response, cancel, no-show, waitlist, missing fields).
- Cause-and-effect chain: Website → Clients → Activities → Follow-up → Analytics → AI.

**Number assertions (examples that must hold as DB truth)**
- 34 going = 34 registrations against 42 capacity (8 spots left) on Website Friday event.
- 17 need attention = 17 records (not a caption).
- AI actions only what seed can prove — no fantasy dataset.

**Brutal QA**: walk backward AI → Analytics → Follow-up → Activity → Client → acquisition; if the chain snaps, cinema is lying. Write five arc fixtures before mount polish.

## Cohestra AI gate

MUST: Cohestra AI chapter ships only if a real committed product intelligence surface exists. Never cinema-only AI. Hold the chapter until product truth exists. Contract: only seed-provable actions; no clairvoyant %; no contact channel the record doesn't support. Same law as Website barely-Cohestra — cinema never invents a Monday the product cannot inhabit.

## Acceptance checklist (suspiciously real + 5 shipping truths + continuity QA)

**Suspiciously real**
- [ ] Arm's-length squint: marketing mount indistinguishable from Monday dashboard.
- [ ] Visitor feels already furnished with *their* week — not a tourist in a demo.
- [ ] Five-minute hallucination: already running the club on Cohestra.
- [ ] Max operational truth + hierarchy; zero decorative density theater.
- [ ] Replica finishes the sentence; marketing prose on mounts near zero.

**Five shipping truths (fail any = no ship)**
- [ ] **Product** — every room maps to real/committed surface; cinema composes, does not invent.
- [ ] **Dataset** — one DemoClub seed; numbers are DB assertions.
- [ ] **Continuity** — anchors + ambient rules hold; reverse-chain walk passes.
- [ ] **Business** — club-fact voice; proves operator need, not feature decoration.
- [ ] **Mental-move-in** — progressive ownership accelerates imagining their club inside Cohestra.

**Continuity QA**
- [ ] Five anchors traceable Website → … → AI.
- [ ] Website 34 ↔ Clients people ↔ Activities regs/cap continuous.
- [ ] Follow-up urgency explained by history; AI actions respect Marcus/phone and seed limits.
- [ ] Reverse walk AI→…→acquisition does not snap.
