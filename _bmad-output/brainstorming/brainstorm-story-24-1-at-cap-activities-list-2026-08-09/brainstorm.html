Story 24.1 — When the tank is full

Cohestra Activities list · at-cap recovery brainstorm · 2026-08-09

The moment

An operator opens /activities at 50/50 published and 5,000/5,000 monthly registrations. Two red banners scream. Two hundred cards scroll — mostly archived load-test noise. They need to free one slot, not read a billing page.

First Principles

Bedrock truths

One surface — what is blocked, what still works, one next action
Recovery ≠ upgrade — Free a slot means archive/unpublish, not checkout
Publish cap is actionable here — reg cap is informational on the list (cards in 24.2)
Reverse Brainstorming

How we fail the operator

Two alerts → ignored as noise
Free a slot → billing → trust break
Chip filters client-side → pagination lies
URL not synced → refresh loses state
Cross-Pollination

Stolen patterns

Gmail storage full — one bar, one Manage button → compound banner + Review published
GitHub repo limit — suggest archive language
Hospital triage — red = publish blocked now; amber note = sign-ups paused
Lead queue chips — same FilterChip visual language as Clients
