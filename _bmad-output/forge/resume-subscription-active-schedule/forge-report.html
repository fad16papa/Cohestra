B2 — Resume Subscription + Active Schedule

Forged 2026-08-10 · Outcome: HARDENED (brainstorm Option 1 revised)

Verdict

The brainstorm recommendation — auto-release schedule on resume — does not survive. It destroys a valid user path: cancel-to-Basic, then schedule a paid downgrade instead, then Keep subscription to undo Basic while keeping Core.

Surviving design: clear cancel at schedule time + confirm on resume when both states exist. Resume never releases schedule.

Killed

  • Auto-release schedule on every resume
  • Block resume until schedule undone
  • Resume mirrors cancel release path

Locked (load-bearing)

  1. Dual state is real — cancel first, schedule Core second; Stripe keeps both flags today.
  2. Resume clears cancel only — schedule stays; paid change still applies at period end.
  3. Fix at source — scheduling paid change clears cancel_at_period_end before creating schedule.
  4. Confirm on Keep subscription when cancel + pending schedule — no silent surprise.
  5. Orphan cleanup — stale tenant schedule fields cleared when Stripe has no schedule (no release call).

The room

"Keep subscription isn't 'undo everything.' It's 'I don't want Basic.' If they already chose Core at period end, releasing the schedule on resume is vandalism dressed as symmetry." 📋 John (PM) + Stripe Billing Survivor (ad-hoc persona)
"Cancel and resume share infrastructure but not intent. Cancel ends the relationship. Resume continues it. A pending downgrade is still a continuing relationship — just a cheaper one." 🏗️ Winston (Architect)
"The one-click Keep subscription with zero copy while two amber banners scream different futures — that's the UX crack. Confirm dialog is cheaper than a support ticket." 🎨 Sally (UX Designer)
