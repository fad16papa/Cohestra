The pair is an extractable behavioral and token contract for Live Proof Cinema: A1–A7 are locked, contrast tokens are hexed with AA targets, pin math is numeric, and Maya’s four journeys cover the intent’s conversion paths. It is not yet a complete visual contract — mockups/ and wireframes/ are empty, and the memlog’s anti-pattern screenshot is missing from imports/ — so a consumer can implement rules without inventing decisions, but must still invent the pinned composition. Downstream architecture / story-dev can source-extract cleanly for copy, seek/pin/PRM, and a11y; they cannot source-extract the stage layout from a picture.

The accessibility lens shifts that from “invent the pinned composition from ASCII” to do not extract the exploded anatomy. Contrast tokens, Focus≠seek, scrub-debounced live region, PRM→click-tabs, and hash-resets-to-Clients are real floors — keep them. Do not treat the Accessibility Floor as shippable. Two locks fight themselves: (1) pills and stage both top: 6rem / z-20, which re-creates 2.4.11 occlusion the last review made blocking; (2) tablist/tabpanel plus aria-hidden+inert on an undefined “stage,” which can hide FeelingCopy or leave a hidden panel. The live mount then adds a third: real WhatsApp/Publish chrome that must not trap, must not be fake, must inner-scroll, and must not steal the pin’s wheel. Those four cannot be true of one subtree. Conditionally specified, not AA-ready — fix the geometry and the hidden-vs-panel map before extract; otherwise implementers will copy top-24 onto two stickies and inert onto the tabpanel.

Prominently blocking (merged unique): 2 critical + 7 high. Rubric source: 0 critical / 1 high / 7 medium / 18 low. Accessibility source: 2 critical / 6 high / 5 medium / 3 low. The rubric’s sole high is the missing desktop-pin mock.
