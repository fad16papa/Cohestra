# Craft notes — Tally.so marketing
Source: https://tally.so/
Captured for transferable UI/UX patterns (steal craft that fits Cohestra — do not become Tally).

Observable craft (not Cohestra decisions):
- One human thesis headline (“The simplest way…”) before feature taxonomy
- Product mechanic is the proof (“just start typing” / Notion-like) — marketing describes the real tool behavior
- Low visual noise; friendly clarity over enterprise SaaS carnival
- Frictionless try CTA (“No signup required”) sits beside the promise
- Sections are job-shaped (“Just start typing”, “Craft intelligent forms”) not chapter-indexed
- Social proof is light and specific, not wallpaper
- Show what using it feels like; avoid hollow decorative UI twins
