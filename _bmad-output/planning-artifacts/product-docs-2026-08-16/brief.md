# Cohestra Document

A public how-to for operators, written so a careful beginner can run an event without a trainer.

## Why now

Epic 19 hardening should not start while new operators still have no in-product teaching surface. The landing page sells Features. It does not teach the clicks.

## Who

Community operators: club leads, workshop hosts, small teams. English. Mixed digital skill. They need “what do I click next?” not architecture.

## What it is

A marketing-chrome page (`/docs`) with a jump list and 19 short chapters: first ten minutes, sign-in, menu, dashboard, communities, activities, form, publish, guests, clients, website, campaigns, reports, settings, plans, troubleshooting, glossary.

## What it is not

Not legal terms. Not a tenant site page. Not a developer runbook. Not a screenshot album (those stay in the internal manual folder).

## Success

- Landing header shows **Document** next to **Features**.
- A new operator can create, publish, and share an activity using only this page.
- Language stays grade-school: short sentences, steps, tables.

## Next

Ship the page. Keep the internal operator manual for screenshots. Update chapters when product limits or menus change.
