# Forged idea — Atelier landing with depth

## Idea

Make Cohestra’s marketing site feel like a **community atelier** — warm, tactile, trustworthy — without sacrificing the operator clarity of pricing tables.

## Survived pressure

- **Pricing must match enforcement** — marketing copy already aligns with `TenantPlanLimits` (250/500/5000 regs; 1/3/10 seats; 4/12/50 activities). Drift risk is layout, not numbers.
- **3D is decoration, not navigation** — ambient scene is `aria-hidden`, pointer-events none.
- **Cookies = consent + charm** — legal strip for trust; disc shapes for human warmth at events.

## Killed

- Replacing pricing table with animated 3D cards (hurts comparison shopping).
- Hiding Enterprise tier on mobile (operators need full picture).

## Act on

1. Align card CTAs vertically
2. Add ambient scene + consent
3. Keep Fraunces + lagoon/gold tokens
