- (decision) Canonical Paddle customer email is Tenant.AdminContactEmail, not signed-in operator email.
- (decision) Signed-in operator email is authorization identity only via ValidateBillingAccessAsync.
- (capability) CAP-1: Create/find Paddle customer using Tenant.AdminContactEmail.
- (capability) CAP-2: When Paddle customer exists and email drifted, update SAME customer to AdminContactEmail.
- (capability) CAP-3: Preserve PaddleCustomerId and PaddleSubscriptionId across reconciliation.
- (capability) CAP-4: Invited TenantAdmin who is not billing owner cannot change Paddle recipient on paid Core/Pro.
- (capability) CAP-5: UpdateBillingContact does not write operator or request email as Paddle recipient; does not transfer AdminContactEmail.
- (capability) CAP-6: Settings Billing copy presents workspace billing owner email read-only.
- (constraint) Do not weaken TenantBillingAccess: paid Core/Pro requires operator == AdminContactEmail; TenantMember 403.
- (constraint) Never create a second Paddle customer or subscription because another admin is signed in.
- (constraint) No live Paddle sandbox mutation, no production credentials, Story 19.4 stays OPEN.
- (constraint) Do not spoof Paddle email delivery; Cohestra only aligns provider customer email.
- (constraint) If live UAT AdminContactEmail later equals observed operator, stop and classify data vs ownership; do not remap to a third address.
- (non-goal) Owner-transfer UX, live UAT Paddle email correction, Story 19.4 close, production deploy.
- (assumption) Live UAT CreativoRare AdminContactEmail was not readable here; implementation binds to the field the owner named as source of truth.
- (decision) ConfirmPlanChange is an authorized reconciliation point and must sync Paddle customer email to AdminContactEmail without changing subscription id.
- (decision) Settings billing email is not freely editable; name/phone remain editable on the same Paddle customer.
